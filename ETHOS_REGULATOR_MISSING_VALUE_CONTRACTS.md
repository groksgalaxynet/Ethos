# ETHOS++ Regulator Missing-Value Contracts

This document contains only owner decisions required before further regulator implementation. No answer below may be inferred from the current repository.

1. **Sloth — required work:** What explicit, source-attributed fact establishes that a task/check is required rather than optional, deferred, delegated with approval, or impossible? Who may assert it, and what scope, expiry, and verification evidence are required?

2. **Sloth — non-completion:** What measurable event establishes that a named, required obligation was abandoned or left incomplete? Is a caller assertion sufficient, or must it come from an execution/outcome or external task-state producer? How are unavailable tools and authorized reassignment handled?

3. **Wrath — action relation:** Which closed, evidence-backed categories distinguish advocacy of escalation/harm/coercion from quotation, historical/fictional description, analysis, prevention, question, defense, or a request for authorization? Which category values can affect a result?

4. **Wrath — target and force context:** What target types, threat-time states, authorization facts, proportionality facts, and consent facts may be used? For each, what source can establish it, and is an asserted claim sufficient?

5. **Wrath — policy:** Are ESI, HPV, and FAF informational observations or enforcement inputs? If enforcement inputs, what exact score/threshold or deterministic rule, policy IDs, precedence, and unknown/conflict behavior are authorized?

6. **Lust — operational subject:** What closed vocabulary defines a relevant stimulation/experience, and what distinguishes it from ordinary exploration, design, accessibility, research, testing, or intensity adjustment? What evidence source may establish that classification?

7. **Lust — decision rule:** Are novelty pursuit and intensity escalation measurements, informational context, or enforcement inputs? What exact values, thresholds, controls, and provenance are allowed?

8. **Gluttony — resource facts:** Which explicit facts define resource identity, owner/shared scope, amount/unit, capacity/quota, stated need, authorization, and redundancy purpose? Which can be caller assertions and which require a trusted quota/ownership/capacity producer?

9. **Gluttony — excess:** Is excess relative to an absolute threshold, stated need, allocated quota, baseline usage, other actors, or time? What evidence establishes that comparison, and how are backup, fault tolerance, approved load testing, retries, and replication excluded?

10. **Gluttony / Greed boundary:** When an action both acquires control of a resource and then consumes/depletes it, should both regulators preserve independent observations, and what—if any—central policy determines priority or joint handling?
